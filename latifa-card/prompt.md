# Latifa El Amrani: photorealistic portrait prompt

The card in `index.html` ships with an illustrated portrait. To get a photorealistic version,
generate the left-hand portrait with an image model (Midjourney, DALL·E, Imagen, Flux, SDXL…)
using the prompt below. Save it as `portrait.jpg` in this folder, then run `node render.js`. The
photo is placed over the illustration automatically.

**Aspect ratio:** about 1:1 (the portrait area is 1040×1080). Keep her face in the upper-middle
of the frame. The right edge fades into the navy panel.

## Prompt

> Photorealistic portrait of an 82-year-old Moroccan woman, a retired civil engineer. Very tall
> and heavyset, broad shoulders, confident upright posture. Warm tan skin with deep wrinkles and
> crow's feet around sharp, intelligent dark eyes. Silver-white hair in a neat bun, partly covered
> by an olive-green silk headscarf. Loose dark navy djellaba with gold buttons down the front,
> thick-framed reading glasses on a thin gold chain, a mechanical pencil in the chest pocket,
> one hand resting on a carved cedar walking cane. Slight sarcastic smirk, strong and slightly
> tired expression. Background: Moroccan zellige-tiled archway, a glass of mint tea on a small
> wooden table, soft golden-hour light. Shot on an 85mm lens, f/1.8, shallow depth of field,
> creamy bokeh, natural skin texture, ultra realistic, editorial portrait photography.

## Negative prompt (for models that support it)

> cartoon, illustration, 3d render, plastic skin, airbrushed, extra fingers, deformed hands,
> text, watermark, logo, oversaturated
